How to measure

  • Chest: measure straight across the garment, 1 inch below the armhole, then double it.
  • Length: measure from the highest shoulder point to the hem.
  • Waist: measure around the waistband without stretching it.
  • Outseam: measure from the top of the waistband to the bottom hem.

Sample measurements should be replaced with final measurements from your production garments before launch.
