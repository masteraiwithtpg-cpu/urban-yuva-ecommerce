# Urban Yuva Ecommerce Website — Project Brief

## Brand

- **Store name:** Urban Yuva
- **Category:** Men's clothing
- **Target customer:** College students aged 18–24

## Products

- **Launch catalogue:** 5–10 products
- **Price range:** ₹499–₹999
- **Product photos:** Professional product photos are available and will replace the launch placeholders

## Look and Feel

- **Style:** Minimal streetwear
- **Colour direction:** Black and white
- **Typography:** Bold, modern fonts
- **Visual reference:** Nike-style clean design

## Pages

- Home
- Shop
- Product details
- Cart and checkout
- About Us
- Contact
- FAQ
- Shipping and Returns
- Size Guide
- Order Tracking

## Payments and Contact

- **Customer contact:** WhatsApp, email, and contact form
- Exact WhatsApp number, email address, and UPI ID will be added before launch

- Offers banner

- Customer reviews

- Product filters
