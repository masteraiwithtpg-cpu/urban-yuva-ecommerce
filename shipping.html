Shipping & returns

Simple timelines and a straightforward way to request help.

01

Dispatch

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 working days. You will receive confirmation on WhatsApp after your order is accepted.

02

Delivery

Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch. Remote areas may take slightly longer.

  • Free above ₹999
  • ₹79 below ₹999
  • India delivery only at launch

03

Returns

Request a return or size exchange on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery.

  • Item must be unused and unwashed
  • Original tags must be attached
  • Final-sale items may not be returnable

How to request a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order ID.
  2. Share the item name, reason and a clear photo.
  3. Wait for pickup or return instructions.
  4. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method or agreed account after inspection.

These are launch-ready sample policies. Review courier charges and your business terms before publishing.
